## 👋 About Me

I’m **Asad Khan**, a Computer Science graduate from **Bacha Khan University, Pakistan**, with a **CGPA of 3.71/4.00**. I’m interested in developing and evaluating AI methods that address real-world problems, especially those related to **honeybee health, smart beekeeping, and precision agriculture**.

My perspective combines computer science research with a personal background in beekeeping. I’m particularly interested in how audio analysis and computer vision can help monitor hives and identify potential threats in a non-invasive, data-driven way.

### 01 · Queen Bee Presence Detection

**WaveNet-Based Queen Bee Presence Detection from Beehive Acoustic Signals: A Comparative Evaluation with CNN and Multimodal Fusion**

This study explores acoustic-signal classification for queen-bee presence, comparing raw-audio and spectrogram-based approaches and examining model fusion for hive monitoring.

### 02 · Varroa Destructor Detection

**Class-Imbalance-Aware Deep Semantic Segmentation for Varroa Destructor Detection Under 780-nm Narrow-Spectrum Illumination**

This work investigates pixel-level segmentation for detecting *Varroa destructor*, a parasite that threatens honeybee colonies, using deep-learning models and narrow-spectrum imagery.
